These pages document the open-source [AgentsOfMine collector](https://github.com/AgentsOfMine/collector). They are written and maintained by the AgentsOfMine project.

## How a page is made

Each page says at the top what it was checked against and on which day. A claim comes from one of three places: the collector's source (with a command you can run), a file on a real install, or the vendor's own documentation. Where a claim could not be checked, the page says so. Some pages are drafted with AI assistance; every command and claim is checked and edited by a person before it is published.

If something here is wrong, [open an issue](https://github.com/AgentsOfMine/collector/issues). We fix it and note the change.

## What this site records

This site sets no cookies, runs no scripts of its own and loads nothing from other websites: fonts and images come from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so **GitHub** serves every request. We do not receive logs or visit counts from GitHub, and we do not add analytics.

GitHub's privacy statement says it logs, for its services, the IP address, the pages viewed and the referring site. I have not verified whether and how that applies to a Pages site, so treat GitHub as able to see that you visited. Read [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for what it says.

The product website, [agentsofmine.io](https://agentsofmine.io), and the [blog](https://blog.agentsofmine.io) each have their own privacy page.
